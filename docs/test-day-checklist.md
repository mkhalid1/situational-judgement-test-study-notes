# Test-day checklist

A short list to run through before and during the real test. Most SJT marks lost on the day come from rushing the scenario, not from poor judgement.

## The day before

- Check the deadline in your invitation and plan to finish well before it.
- Find out the format: rating, ranking, most and least, or a mix. Reread that format's guide.
- Read the role description or job advert again, including any values or Behaviours it lists.
- Do five practice items in your test's format, then stop.
- Sleep. An SJT needs careful reading more than anything else.

## One hour before

- Charge or plug in your laptop. Use a stable internet connection.
- Close other tabs and silence notifications.
- Have water nearby. If the test uses video, have headphones ready.
- If the test is untimed, block out more time than you think you need. GOV.UK suggests at least an hour for the Civil Service test.

## In the first minute

- Read every instruction screen. Check the scale labels and whether ratings can be repeated.
- Check whose point of view you are answering from: yours in the role, or a named person's.
- Use the practice questions if they are offered.

## During the test

For every scenario:

1. **Role:** who am I in this scenario, and what am I allowed to do?
2. **Risk:** who could be harmed, and how soon?
3. **Rule:** is a policy or standard mentioned?
4. **Five checks** on each option: problem, people, rules, role, timing.

And throughout:

- Answer as a capable person doing the job well, not as you might on a bad day.
- When torn between two adjacent ratings, pick one and move on. Close answers usually earn partial credit.
- Never leave an item blank.
- If you are timed, keep an eye on the clock at the halfway point.
- Do not ask anyone for help. It breaks the rules of most tests, including the Civil Service test.

## Afterwards

- Write down which scenario types felt hardest. It helps for the next test and for interviews.
- If an interview follows, prepare real examples for the qualities the SJT covered.
- For the Civil Service test, remember that feedback is automatic and does not cover specific answers.

## Last practice before the real one

If your test is in a few days, do one short timed set in the [free situational judgement practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/sjt?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=situational-judgement-test), review the misses with the three questions in the [study plans](study-plans.md#how-to-review-a-missed-item), and then rest.
