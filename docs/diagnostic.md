# 15-minute diagnostic

Eight original items, two for each SJT format, to show you where to start. Set a timer for 8 minutes, answer everything, then check your answers and read the table at the bottom.

These items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from any employer's or publisher's test. They are keyed against the five checks explained in the [most and least guide](sections/most-and-least-effective.md#the-scoring-frame-used-in-these-notes): deal with the real problem, keep people safe and treat them with respect, be honest and follow the rules, stay within your role and involve the right person, and act in good time.

If you already know the formats, skip this and take the [free situational judgement practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/sjt?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=xt9w5q&utm_content=situational-judgement-test) instead.

## Part A: Most and least effective

Choose the **most effective** and the **least effective** option.

**D1.** You have just joined a team. Your manager gives you a task using software you have never used, due tomorrow.

- A. Ask your manager or a colleague for a quick walkthrough, and start on the parts you can already do.
- B. Spend the evening watching tutorials without telling anyone, hoping to finish in time.
- C. Say nothing, and on the deadline tell your manager you did not do it because you did not know how.
- D. Do it in a different program you know, without checking whether that is acceptable.

**D2.** You are a hospital porter. A visitor asks you which ward a named patient is on, and what they are in hospital for.

- A. Tell them both, since they know the patient's name.
- B. Direct them to the main reception or the ward staff, who can check what can be shared.
- C. Tell them the ward, but not the reason.

## Part B: Ranking

Rank the four options from 1 (most effective) to 4 (least effective).

**D3.** A colleague tells you they are struggling with stress and finding their workload overwhelming.

- A. Listen, and encourage them to talk to their manager or the employee support service.
- B. Tell them everyone is stressed at the moment and they will be fine.
- C. Tell the rest of the team, so everyone can go easy on them.
- D. Offer to help with one specific task this week.

**D4.** You work in a customer contact centre. You realise that for the past week you have been giving customers the wrong deadline for refunds, because the policy changed and you missed the update.

- A. Tell your team leader, so the affected customers can be contacted with the correct information.
- B. Give the correct information from now on and say nothing about the past week.
- C. Look through your call notes and contact the affected customers yourself, without telling your team leader.
- D. Keep giving the old deadline so that your answers stay consistent.

## Part C: Rating effectiveness

> You work in a government contact centre. A colleague has started sending you their unfinished cases to complete, saying they are too busy. It is now affecting your own work.

Rate each action: Effective, Fairly Effective, Ineffective or Counterproductive.

**D5.** Keep doing their cases, but complain about them to other colleagues.

**D6.** Talk to the colleague, explain the effect on your own work, and agree what you can and cannot take on.

## Part D: Rating importance

> Nadia is a medical student. A consultant has asked her to observe a procedure. When she walks in, she sees that the patient is a former teacher of hers, who looks uncomfortable to see her.

How important are each of the following for Nadia in deciding whether to stay? Choose Very important, Important, Of minor importance, or Not important at all.

**D7.** The patient's wishes about who is present.

**D8.** The consultant's request that Nadia observe.

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations</summary>

**D1: Most A, least C.** A asks for help early and gets moving. C hides the problem until it is too late to fix. *Why not D as least:* it might produce something unusable, but at least work is done.

**D2: Most B, least A.** B gets the visitor to people who can check what can be shared. A discloses medical information to someone whose relationship to the patient you do not know. *Why not C as most:* even the ward can be private, and staff are better placed to check.

**D3: A, D, B, C.** A listens and points them to support that can deal with the cause. D is real, practical help, but smaller. B is dismissive but harmless. C shares something personal without their permission.

**D4: A, C, B, D.** A gets the error fixed for every affected customer, with your team leader involved. C fixes it for customers but your team leader cannot check the scale of the problem or tell others. B stops new harm but leaves a week of customers with wrong information. D knowingly keeps giving wrong information.

**D5: Counterproductive.** The problem continues and you damage trust in the team by complaining behind their back.

**D6: Effective.** It deals with the problem directly, honestly and politely.

**D7: Very important.** Patients have a right to decide who is present during their care.

**D8: Important.** Nadia should take a supervisor's request seriously, but it does not outweigh the patient's wishes.

</details>

## What your result means

| Part | Items | Format guide |
|---|---|---|
| A | D1 to D2 (4 marks: most and least) | [Most and least](sections/most-and-least-effective.md) |
| B | D3 to D4 (8 marks: one per option placed correctly) | [Ranking](sections/ranking-responses.md) |
| C | D5 to D6 (2 marks) | [Rating effectiveness](sections/rating-effectiveness.md) |
| D | D7 to D8 (2 marks) | [Rating importance](sections/rating-importance.md) |

- **Start with the part where you dropped the most marks, as a share of the total.** For example, 1 out of 2 in Part D is weaker than 6 out of 8 in Part B.
- **If you missed several "least" choices**, read the traps in the [most and least guide](sections/most-and-least-effective.md#traps-that-cost-points) first. Choosing "do nothing" over "cause harm" is the most common slip.
- **If you scored well everywhere**, go straight to timed practice and the [study plans](study-plans.md).
