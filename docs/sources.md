## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not statements from any test provider, and are labelled as guidance where they appear:

- the five checks and how they map onto each rating scale
- the general description of how SJT keys are built from expert panels
- per-item timing targets and the 80% practice target
- the step-by-step methods, traps lists, study plans and checklists
- all practice items, keys and explanations, which are original
